## About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at CVR College of Engineering who enjoys building products that connect strong engineering with real user problems.

My work usually sits at the intersection of full-stack development, GenAI-powered products, real-time systems, and Web3 experiments. I like taking ideas from "this might work" to a usable product quickly, especially in hackathon-style environments where clarity, speed, and execution matter.

- Currently working as a Project Intern at Oracle, building automation and enterprise workflow improvements for utility billing systems.
- Previously built and deployed full-stack React, Node.js, and MongoDB applications at Digital Guruji.
- Interested in product-minded engineering, developer tools, AI workflows, decentralized systems, and scalable backend design.
